## Reconstructed rules

**Setup**
- Pull out four cards of each rank you need (based on player count), e.g. for
  4 players: all four A's, four 2's, four 3's, four 4's, plus 1 Joker → 17 cards total.
- Shuffle thoroughly.
- Deal: one player gets 5 cards, everyone else gets 4 cards each (the extra
  card/Joker is what makes the passing phase work).

**Passing phase**
- Starting with the player holding 5 cards, each player picks one card they
  don't want/need and passes it to the next player, simultaneously, every round.
- This repeats continuously around the table.
- Everyone keeps their hand secret — the goal is to silently collect four
  cards of the same rank (a four-of-a-kind "set").

**Win / slap phase**
- The moment a player completes a four-of-a-kind, they slap the table
  (instead of announcing it out loud, or optionally shouting first).
- Everyone else must notice and slap the table too, as fast as possible.
- Whoever is **last** to slap gets a penalty, chosen/assigned by the winning
  player (the one who completed the set).
- Escalation variants shown at the end:
  - "Super Pressure": the **last two** players to slap both get penalized.
  - "Ultimate Super Pressure": penalty escalates further — the winning player
    (who got the four-of-a-kind) is exempt from any penalty.

This is functionally the classic card game **"Pig"** (a.k.a. "Donkey"/"Squeal"
variants), just using a table-slap instead of grabbing a spoon/object in the
middle, with an added social "assign your own punishment" twist and stacking
pressure variants for extra players.
